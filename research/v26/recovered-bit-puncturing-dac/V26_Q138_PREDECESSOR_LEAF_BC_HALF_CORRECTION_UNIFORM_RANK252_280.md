# V26 Q138 predecessor-leaf B/C half correction: uniform rank 252 / 280

## Scope

This result concerns only the four weight-122 half-integral sectors in the B/C second dyadic residue. It is a uniform statement over every predecessor-input assignment for which the common support is active and over all right-side assignments.

It is **not** a full B/C second-residue theorem and is **not** a rational-rank claim for the actual 0/1 correction matrix.

## Local Boolean correction

For the four half-sector sign bits `q_1,...,q_4`, the second-bit correction is

`h(q)=1 + sum_i q_i + sum_{i<j} q_i q_j` over `F_2`.

For a fixed predecessor input and right assignment each sign bit, as a function of the 11 S1-left variables, has the form

`q_i = Q_i + a_i`,

where `Q_i` is a fixed quadratic truth vector and `a_i` lies in an affine-function space generated by right cross terms and predecessor-input shifts.

Exact computation gives affine-delta dimension 12 for each of the four sectors in both B and C.

By distributivity over `F_2`, the family of all possible `h(q)` is contained in the span generated by

- `1`;
- `Q_i` and the four affine-delta spaces;
- for every pair `i<j`, `Q_i Q_j`, `Q_i A_j`, `A_i Q_j`, and `A_i A_j`.

The unrestricted left truth-vector spans have dimensions

- B: 88;
- C: 97.

## Common support

The common support has eight stored affine equations. Their left-mask rank is 2 and right-mask rank is 3. Allowing every right syndrome and every predecessor-input translation gives exactly four nonzero left support cosets.

Multiplying the unrestricted half-correction span by all four possible left support masks and taking the exact `F_2` span gives

`dim <= 252` for B,

`dim <= 280` for C.

This deliberately over-approximates correlations between input shifts, right syndromes, and phase shifts, so the bound is uniform.

Therefore the half-sector second-correction matrices satisfy

`rank_F2(H_B) <= 252`,

`rank_F2(H_C) <= 280`

for every fixed predecessor input.

Any binary matrix of `F_2` rank `r` has an integer lift congruent to it modulo 2 with rational rank at most `r`: choose an `F_2` rank factorization `UV`, interpret the 0/1 factors over `Z`, and use the integer product `UV`. Hence these bounds may be used as dyadic correction-lift envelopes 252 and 280.

## Relation to fixed-input probes

Exact full right scans previously found ranks 128 for B and 136 for C on a canonical active input and three independent active-coset shifts. Those are fixed-input witnesses only. The present 252/280 theorem is the admitted uniform envelope.

## Consequence for the second residue

The already admitted support-only second-residue envelopes are 668 for B and 788 for C. Combining only the support-only and half-sector pieces gives partial envelopes

`668 + 252 = 920` for B,

`788 + 280 = 1068` for C.

The e0 negative-sign assembly remains unresolved, so 920/1068 are **not** complete B/C second-residue bounds.
