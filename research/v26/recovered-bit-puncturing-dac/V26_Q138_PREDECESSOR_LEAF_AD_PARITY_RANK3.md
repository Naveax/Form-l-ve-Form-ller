# V26 q138 predecessor-leaf A/D first 2-adic parity rank

## Exact statement

Let `S1={0,1,2,3,4,5,12,13,14,15,16}` and let `L_A(beta)` and `L_D(beta)` be exact predecessor q138 leaf coefficient vectors with one open output word at position A or D.

The dyadic-descent theorem gives `L_A,L_D in 2^-92 Z`. Define integer-valued vectors

`M_A=2^92 L_A`, `M_D=2^92 L_D`.

For every fixed external input-mask assignment, the modulo2 matricization across `S1 | S1^c` satisfies

`rank_F2(M_A mod2) <=3`,

`rank_F2(M_D mod2) <=3`.

This is a statement about the first 2-adic residue only. It does not imply rational Schmidt rank at most3.

## Surviving carry sectors modulo2

For A/D all j4 carries are identically zero, so total carry weight is at most93.

After scaling by `2^92` and reducing modulo2:

- all weights at most91 vanish;
- at weight92, among the93 one-zero patterns in j1..j3,91 have internal rank127 and two-point fibers, hence even signed numerators and vanish modulo2;
- exactly two weight92 sectors have internal rank128: `(j1 bit0)` and `(j3 bit0)`;
- weight93 has internal rank127 and one-dimensional nullspace. Its contribution divided by2 is odd exactly when the two fiber signs are equal. The sign derivative along the null vector is affine-linear, so this condition adds one affine GF(2) equation to the sector consistency system.

Thus the first parity residue is the XOR of at most three affine-subspace indicator functions of beta.

## Communication rank of each indicator

Eliminate the internal masks symbolically while retaining all fixed-input bits and beta as external GF(2) variables.

For each of the two weight92 unique-solution sectors, and for the weight93 sector augmented by the sign-derivative-equals-zero equation, the resulting affine consistency system has the property that the beta-column images generated by S1 and by its complement have zero intersection.

For an affine indicator `1[A x + B y = c]`, its GF(2) communication-matrix rank across x|y is `2^dim(im A intersect im B)`. Hence each of the three surviving indicators has rank1.

Subadditivity gives the uniform bound3.
