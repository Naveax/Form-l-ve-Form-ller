## Four valuation-e3 regimes

By the admitted A/D arbitrary zero-set internal-rank law, direct valuation e=3 consists of:

1. `N`: three nonspecial zero sites, internal rank127/nullity1;
2. `S1`: special `(1,0)` plus three nonspecial zero sites, internal rank128;

For regime N, every all-nonspecial zero set has the same one-dimensional internal kernel generated by the top direction. Nonzero equal-sign Gauss summation imposes the corresponding radical derivative equation. Extra nonspecial zero equalities may then be dropped for an upper support bound.

For S1/S3/B, impose only the relevant special zero equality rows and drop all nonspecial zero equalities. Again this enlarges support safely.

Let `P_N,P_S1,P_S3,P_B` be the four linear predecessor-to-left particular maps. A common shift `P_N x` does not affect union cardinality. Consider the joint relative map

`x -> ((P_S1+P_N)x, (P_S3+P_N)x, (P_B+P_N)x)`

Its exact GF(2) rank is only

`Rows_N union (Rows_S1+d1) union (Rows_S3+d3) union (Rows_B+dB)`
