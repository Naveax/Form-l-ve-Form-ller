# V26 q138 Right-Map Quotient-Blocks-197 Theorem

## Purpose

`V26_QR_Q138_RIGHT_MAP_REACHABLE_HULL197_THEOREM.md` proves that the formal 1024-dimensional doubled Gram transfer state has an exact 197-dimensional invariant reachable hull `U` for the complete site-3 boundary family and all eight interior control types.

This note gives the canonical block structure of that quotient under exchange of the two Gram copies.

The result is

`U = U_+ direct_sum U_-`

with exact dimensions

`138 + 59 = 197`,

and every interior transfer is block diagonal in this decomposition.

## 1. Copy-exchange involution

Write a doubled hidden state as `(a,b)` with `a,b in {0,1}^5` and define

`S(a,b)=(b,a)`.

Because both Gram copies contain identical exact Walsh tensors and share the same summed parent-external variables, every doubled interior transfer satisfies

`S K_tau = K_tau S`.

This was also checked entry-for-entry for all eight exact rational `1024 x 1024` transfer operators.

Therefore the `+1` and `-1` eigenspaces of S are invariant under every transfer.

## 2. Reachable sector growth

Project the cumulative reachable hull after each interior closure step onto the symmetric and antisymmetric copy sectors.

Exact rational elimination gives the sector dimensions

- boundary only: `(20,8)`;
- after one closure step: `(107,43)`;
- after two: `(135,58)`;
- after three: `(138,59)`;
- after the next closure: `(138,59)` again.

Thus the common invariant reachable hull is

`dim U_+ = 138`,

`dim U_- = 59`.

No further copy-sector direction is generated by any of the eight local control operators.

## 3. Exact block ranks

Use lexicographic control order

`000,001,010,011,100,101,110,111`.

On the 138-dimensional symmetric block, the exact operator ranks are

`136, 89, 63, 66, 80, 87, 68, 56`.

On the 59-dimensional antisymmetric block, the exact ranks are

`58, 37, 26, 28, 32, 35, 32, 24`.

Adding the two block ranks gives

`194, 126, 89, 94, 112, 122, 100, 80`,

which exactly reproduces the ranks of the eight operators restricted to the full 197-dimensional reachable hull.

Hence the quotient transfer algebra is genuinely block diagonal and no rank is hidden in cross-sector coupling.

## 4. Exact quotient form

Choose any exact rational bases of `U_+` and `U_-` obtained from the reachable hull.

Every interior transfer can then be represented as

`K_tau|_U = K_tau^(+) direct_sum K_tau^(-)`,

where

- `K_tau^(+)` is at most `138 x 138`;
- `K_tau^(-)` is at most `59 x 59`.

The original formal doubled transfer had size `1024 x 1024`.

Therefore exact right-map rank propagation can be performed in two much smaller invariant systems rather than in the ambient doubled state.

This is a structural reduction, not a numerical truncation.

## 5. Why this matters

The local ambient operators are not uniformly low rank; `K_000` has ambient rank 1016. The compression appears only after imposing the actual boundary/source family and taking its invariant closure.

The sequence is therefore

`formal operator state 1024`

`-> boundary-conditioned invariant hull 197`

`-> canonical copy sectors 138 + 59`.

This is the precise sense in which the q138 rank-conditioning problem is source conditioned: irrelevant ambient directions are never generated by the allowed boundary family, even though the full local operator can act almost invertibly on them.
