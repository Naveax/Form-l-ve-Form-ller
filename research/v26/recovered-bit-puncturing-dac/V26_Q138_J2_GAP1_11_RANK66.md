# V26 Q138 j2 gap bits1..11 exact rank66 theorem

## Scope

This isolates the only five S1 physical bits left raw after the clean 39-bit natural-bridge theorem:

`C1,C2,C3,C4,C5`.

They sit on the complementary j2 carry arc between the bit0/wrap side and the C12-side block. The exact local gap operator keeps both endpoint carries `s0,s11` as row/interface variables.

This theorem is a local interface-rank result. It does **not** by itself lower the canonical central S1 rank, because the 39-bit incoming factor may or may not reach the 62-dimensional local kernel.

## Gap operator

Let

`M : F^{C1..C5,s0,s11} -> F^{retained gap variables}`.

The row/domain dimension is

`2^(5+2)=128`.

Retained columns are:

- bits1..5: `(v_i,w_i)`, ten binary coordinates;
- bits6..11: `(C_i,v_i,w_i)`, eighteen binary coordinates.

Thus the explicit column space has `2^28` assignments.

For each bit the exact Walsh coefficient is the standard interior tensor

`T(s_i,s_{i-1},C_i,v_i,w_i)`.

Internal carries `s1..s10` are contracted.

## Exact Gram dynamic program

Rather than materializing the `128 x 2^28` matrix, compute

`G = M M^T`.

For a pair of rows, only the two carry states from the two copies must be propagated. Therefore the Gram transfer state has four states `(s,s')`.

At a physical-C bit `i=1..5`, the row pair fixes `(C_i,C'_i)` and the transfer sums over the shared retained `(v_i,w_i)`.

At a retained-C bit `i=6..11`, the transfer sums over shared `(C_i,v_i,w_i)`.

Multiplying five physical-C transfers and six retained-C transfers gives every entry of the exact `128 x 128` rational Gram matrix while implicitly summing all `2^28` retained columns.

Exact Fraction Gaussian elimination gives

`rank_Q(G)=66`.

Over Q/R,

`rank(M M^T)=rank(M)`, so

`rank_Q(M)=66`.

Hence the complete gap operator has

`rank = 66 / 128`,

`kernel dimension = 62`.

This is the first genuine rank deficiency found on the complete natural S1 carry bridge. It is not a sampling artefact and does not depend on a modular lower-bound witness.

## Interpretation

The previous natural-bridge pieces were universally injective or reproduced their product geometry exactly. Bits1..11 are different: connecting both endpoint carries creates a real local kernel.

However the D3 lesson applies again. A local kernel is useful only if the actual incoming boundary row space reaches it.

Let `W39` denote the exact 39-bit merged natural factor, viewed on boundary carry coordinates `(s0,s11)` plus its other retained interfaces. The next required object is therefore the intersection of

`F^{C1..C5} tensor W39`

with `ker(M)` under contraction of the two endpoint carry legs.

Until that boundary/fiber geometry is proved, the canonical d=1 bound remains

`rank_center(S1) <= 171*2^34`,

`W_repr(1) <= 78+log2(171)`.
