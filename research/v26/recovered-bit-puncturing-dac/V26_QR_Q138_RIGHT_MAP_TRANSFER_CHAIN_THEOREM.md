# V26 q138 Right-Map Five-Site Transfer-Chain Theorem

## Purpose

`V26_QR_Q138_RIGHT_MAP_RANK_CONDITIONING_FALSIFIER.md` showed that the 107-leaf right-map rank depends on fixed input-mask choices. This note explains why that dependence is still tractable: the 107-leaf subnetwork is not an arbitrary graph. It is an exact five-site finite-state transfer chain.

The result replaces a nominal 17-bit brute-force rank-control problem by a sequence of local transfer operators with only five binary hidden state bits between neighboring sites.

## 1. Right-map geometry

In the certified 171-leaf width-40 bottleneck, the right child contains 107 tensor leaves and maps the six child-interface bits to 24 parent-external bits.

Group its factors into sites `i=3,4,5,6,7` as follows.

For each site i, include the local T1/T2/T3/T4 addition-bit factors at bit i, the local parity factor `P_i`, and the shifted T4 factors at bit `i+8` that are coupled by the ChaCha ROR8 wiring.

The final site also contains the wrap/boundary fragments at bit 31 and the T2/T4 fragments that terminate at the six-bit child interface.

The resulting exact site sizes are

- site 3: 18 tensor leaves;
- site 4: 21;
- site 5: 21;
- site 6: 21;
- site 7: 26.

Total: `18+21+21+21+26 = 107`.

## 2. Five-bit hidden state

For every prefix cut after site `i=3,4,5,6`, the only tensor indices connecting the processed and unprocessed sites are exactly

- `sig4_i`;
- `sig4_{i+8}`;
- `sig3_i`;
- `sig2_i`;
- `sig1_i`.

All five are binary.

Therefore the exact one-copy right map has an inter-site hidden state of dimension

`2^5 = 32`.

No trail enumeration, sigma cap or approximation is used.

This is a factor-graph theorem: the right map is a five-site hidden-state chain whose state is the tuple of five surviving carry/sigma bits.

## 3. Gram transfer state

To obtain the exact interface rank, use the Gram tensor

`G_R(z,z') = sum_x R(z,x) R(z',x)`.

The doubled network contains two copies of the five-bit hidden state, so the exact Gram transfer state has dimension

`2^(5+5) = 1024`.

Thus the entire fixed-mask rank problem can be written schematically as

`G_R = B_L * K_3 * K_4 * K_5 * K_6 * K_7 * B_R`,

where each `K_i` is a finite exact transfer operator on the doubled five-bit state, with additional endpoint/interface legs only at the boundaries.

The huge explicit Walsh/trail support is irrelevant to this transfer-state dimension.

## 4. Local control bits

For interior sites `i=3..7`, the fixed-mask dependence enters locally through the triple

`tau_i = (u1_i, u2_i, p_i)`,

where

- `u1_i` is the fixed T1 ordinary input-mask bit;
- `u2_i` is the fixed T2 ordinary input-mask bit;
- `p_i` is the affine parity/XOR constant of the local P relation.

There are therefore only eight local transfer types per ordinary site.

Additional fixed T2 bits at the wrap/interface endpoints are boundary controls rather than new bulk site types.

This is the key reduction: instead of viewing the right-map rank as an opaque function of many fixed bits, it is generated by a short product of local transfer operators selected from a finite alphabet.

Exact rational finite calculations provide a useful structural fingerprint.

When only one site triple is varied away from the zero representative, sites 4, 5 and 6 have **identical** exact `(right-rank, parent-rank)` tables:

This exact repetition is explained by the transfer geometry: sites 4,5,6 are copies of the same interior local wiring. Their fixed-bit dependence is therefore governed by the same transfer family `K_tau`.

Sites 3 and 7 differ because they touch the left/right boundary structure.

## 7. Mathematical consequence

The right-map rank-conditioning problem should now be attacked as a finite-state transfer problem, not by repeatedly contracting the full 107-leaf graph for arbitrary mask samples.

The correct next object is the family of exact local doubled transfer operators

`K_tau`, `tau in {0,1}^3`,

plus the two boundary operators.

Once those are materialized algebraically, one can:

1. classify the reachable transfer subspace after each site;
2. compute exact right-map/parent-rank envelopes for all control sequences without re-solving the full tensor network;
3. identify invariant sectors or quotient states that reduce the 1024 doubled state;
4. restrict the final rank law to the q138 source-conditioned/reachable mask family.

This is the mathematically preferred route to the next compression layer.

## 9. Claims not admitted

This theorem does not yet give the worst-case or source-conditioned exact rank envelope, does not prove `W<=39`, and does not establish practical QR evaluation, exact arithmetic-work reduction, double-round/full-round contraction, ranking gain, or `alpha<1`.

It establishes the finite-state mathematical form needed to solve the rank-conditioning problem without reducing the research to brute-force mask enumeration.
